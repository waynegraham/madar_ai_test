Comparison · not an accuracy assessment

Where the models disagree

These examples show different labels on approximately the same area, and regions without a matching counterpart in another system. They describe the saved predictions without deciding which is right. Every crop is taken from the original image; selecting it opens the full-resolution detail.

No label differences or unmatched boxes were found by this comparison rule.

How these examples were selected

All pairs of prediction sets are compared using the existing greedy one-to-one bounding-box matcher. The largest overlap is considered first; a match requires intersection-over-union (IoU) of at least 0.5. Labels do not determine matches, and polygons are not substituted for boxes.

The short selection contains up to four examples. The Qwen variants are compared first, then other system pairs. Within these groups, differing labels precede unmatched boxes; larger unmatched boxes precede smaller ones. Examples overlapping an earlier selection at IoU 0.4 or greater are omitted from the short selection only. The expanded list retains every pairwise label conflict and unmatched region.

For the pair being compared, labels come from the exact matched regions. An unmatched result stays labeled “No matched region,” even if a nearby or differently grouped box overlaps it. Other systems and human-reviewed references are compared independently with the crop area at the same IoU threshold. Human references do not generate disagreement examples.

A missing counterpart is not necessarily a missed feature: a system may have grouped or bounded it differently. Human-drawn boxes without recorded review are not substituted for a human-reviewed reference.

From one manuscript to the corpus

The collection contains approximately 400 manuscripts, generally 4–10 pages each. A feature noticed on one page could become a starting point for asking questions across the collection.

If features could be extracted reliably and reviewed, individual pages could become a research corpus linked by marks, passages, names, and questions. The pathways below are possibilities to investigate—not searches or historical connections already proven by this experiment.
