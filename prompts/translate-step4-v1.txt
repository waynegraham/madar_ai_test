CONFIDENCE SCORING

Confidence scores indicate your confidence in your own analysis,
not the probability that the text is objectively correct.

Use values from 0.0 to 1.0.

0.90–1.00:
Reading/interpretation is highly conventional and there is little
reasonable ambiguity.

0.70–0.89:
Good evidence supports the interpretation, but alternatives exist.

0.50–0.69:
Interpretation is plausible but materially uncertain.

0.30–0.49:
Interpretation is speculative or depends heavily on context.

0.00–0.29:
Unable to establish the reading or meaning reliably.

Do not increase confidence simply because an interpretation produces
fluent English.

When multiple interpretations are plausible, lower the confidence
of the selected interpretation and record the alternatives.

Never assign high confidence to a word whose source reading is itself
uncertain.
