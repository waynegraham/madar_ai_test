Historical Arabic-script manuscripts

Reading the page, tracing the evidence

A working record of experiments in identifying text, annotations, stamps and seals, and transcribing manuscript passages.

How to read this report

Each entry below represents one manuscript image. A file identifier is not a catalogue description, and several images may belong to the same manuscript.

Human reviewed
Human-corrected material explicitly stored as ground truth. The report identifies any missing information about the reviewer or scope of corrections.
Machine suggested
A proposed reading or identification from a model, including machine-generated eScriptorium output. It is not an established finding.
Not yet reviewed
No completed human review is recorded. Human-drawn layout annotations are identified separately; they do not establish an independently reviewed transcription.

Counts describe what was recorded, not how accurately a system understood the page. Missing evidence is shown as unavailable.
