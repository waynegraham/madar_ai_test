Editorial conventions

Reading this report

The image is evidence. A model’s transcription or identification is a proposal about that evidence. Review labels describe the status recorded in the research files, not a judgment about the quality of a reading.

Human reviewed
Human-corrected material explicitly stored as ground truth. Missing information about the reviewer or scope of corrections is noted.
Machine suggested
A proposed reading or identification, including machine-generated eScriptorium output. It is not an established finding.
Not yet reviewed
No completed review is recorded. Human-drawn regions are identified separately; they do not establish an independently reviewed transcription.

Experimental material. Recorded region counts are inventories, not measures of accuracy. Unavailable evidence is never presented as a negative finding.
